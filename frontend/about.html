01 • PROBLEM STATEMENT & MANDATE

The Leap from 2D Revenue Plots to 3D Volumetric Legal Space

Over 65% of civil court litigation in urban India arises from property boundary disputes, multi-storey flat ownership ambiguities, and underground infrastructure collisions. Traditional land registries operate on colonial 2D polygon surveys that cannot represent vertical property rights, multi-floor condominiums, basement parking easements, or transit air-rights.

VKARMA establishes a unified 3D digital twin cadastre compliant with the Land Administration Domain Model (LADM) standard. Every volumetric space—from a 14th-floor residential flat to an underground utility vault—receives an unforgeable 3D ULPIN tokenized by format-preserving Feistel ciphers and verified with Dihedral D5 Verhoeff checksums.

04 • MATHEMATICS & STANDARDS

The Cryptographic & Geospatial Stack

Feistel Format-Preserving Encryption

Internal serial numbers are transformed into 8-character alphanumeric pseudorandom tokens via a balanced 4-round Feistel cipher. Sequential parcel generation yields completely non-sequential public identifiers, preventing enumeration attacks while preserving exact length.

Dihedral D5 Verhoeff Check Digit

Every 3D ULPIN is protected with a non-commutative dihedral permutation checksum based on the symmetry group $D_5$. This guarantees detection of 100% of single-character transcription errors and 100% of adjacent transposition errors.

Volumetric 3D Cadastre

Implements the international framework for volumetric land administration. Spatial units (`LA_SpatialUnit`), volumetric legal spaces (`LA_LegalSpaceBuildingUnit`), rights, restrictions, and responsibilities (`LA_RRR`), and parties (`LA_Party`) are modeled in exact spatial topology.

Topological Collision Detection

Utilizes the 3D cadastre topological validation algorithms (Jaljolie et al.) to identify volumetric encroachments, interior space overlaps, and coordinate boundary breaches between neighboring legal units in real time.
